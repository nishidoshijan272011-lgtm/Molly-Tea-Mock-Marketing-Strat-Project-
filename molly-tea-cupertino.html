2. Brand

What is Molly Tea?

Molly Tea is a popular tea brand founded in 2021 in Shenzhen, China by Zhang Bochung, which has been expanded internationally over the years. Molly Tea's goal is to expose the world to eastern modern tea and express it through unique branding. The brand has been awarded with certifications such as being the first brand dedicated to floral scents in the brewed tea category by CIC (China Insights Consultantly) and is recognized for its trademark registrations in its leadership. As of right now, it has about 2,000 locations worldwide and is still successful and well-regarded in the market.

3 ❀⋆ Perception

How is Molly Tea presented today?

Molly's Tea image is presenting itself as a culturally rich and globally adaptable tea brand that is artistically driven. People recongize Molly Tea as a Chinese tea brand that is modern and trendy. They have a variety of teas such as milk tea, iced tea, snowy whipped cream tea, fruit tea, floral tea, and matcha. Besides selling tea, they also sell their merchandise such as magnets, gift boxes, and accessories. Molly Tea is also known for its unique and creative packaging, which often features cute and playful designs in pink which is their major color theme, they also have other brands, tap to swatch below!
